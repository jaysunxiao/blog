# Narration Script — HTML Text to Animation

## Chinese

### 01 — 约 15 秒

一段文字，能变成什么？

它可以是一场电影。  
一张会呼吸的海报。  
一座不断生长的城市。  
甚至，一个正在你眼前运行的世界。

现在，只需要从一句话开始。

### 02 — 约 20 秒

这就是 HTML Text to Animation。

一个能够阅读文字、理解表达，并把内容转化为动画的视觉创作技能。

你交给它的，不需要是分镜，也不需要是设计稿。

可以是一段文案、一个概念、一次讲述，甚至，只是一个还没有被看见的想法。

### 03 — 约 23 秒

在它背后，是六百种核心视觉风格。

电影、漫画、剪纸、像素、手绘与三维空间；  
科技界面、品牌发布、动态字体与实验影像；  
数据、商业、地图、建筑、机械、自然、音乐与科学。

从一滴墨的扩散，到一座城市的运转；  
从一个字符的跳动，到一个完整系统的诞生。

几乎每一种内容，都能找到属于自己的视觉世界。

### 04 — 约 20 秒

但它不是在六百套模板中，随机选择一套。

它会先理解：

这段文字为什么存在？  
它要说服谁？  
解释什么？  
传递怎样的情绪？

它寻找的，不是最炫目的效果，而是那个最准确、最有力量的表达。

### 05 — 约 22 秒

随后，文字被拆解成节奏。

概念形成结构，  
结构产生运动，  
运动连接成叙事。

一句重要的话，可以冲破画面；  
一组复杂的数据，可以开始生长；  
一个抽象的机制，可以在眼前被拆开、连接，然后真正运行。

在这里，动画不再是装饰。

动画，就是内容本身。

### 06 — 约 16 秒

最终，这一切会被写进一个完整的 HTML 文件。

不需要时间轴，  
不需要庞大的制作工程。

HTML、CSS、SVG 与 Canvas，共同构成一支可以直接在浏览器中播放的动画作品。

### 07 — 约 14 秒

六百种风格，不是六百种特效。

而是六百种观看世界的方式。

让数据拥有节奏，  
让知识拥有空间，  
让故事拥有光影，  
让那些原本只能被读到的文字——

第一次，真正被看见。

HTML Text to Animation。

从一句话开始，创造一个世界。

## English

### 01 — About 15 seconds

What can a piece of text become?

A film.  
A poster that breathes.  
A city that never stops growing.  
Even an entire world, unfolding before your eyes.

And now, it all begins with a single sentence.

### 02 — About 20 seconds

This is HTML Text to Animation.

A visual creation skill that reads your words, understands their meaning, and transforms them into animation.

You don’t need to provide a storyboard.  
You don’t need a finished design.

Start with a piece of copy, a concept, a story—or simply an idea that has yet to be seen.

### 03 — About 23 seconds

Behind it are six hundred core visual styles.

Cinema, comics, paper cutouts, pixel art, hand-drawn illustration, and three-dimensional space.  
Futuristic interfaces, product launches, kinetic typography, and experimental imagery.  
Data, business, maps, architecture, machinery, nature, music, and science.

From a drop of ink spreading across the page to an entire city in motion.  
From a single character coming alive to the birth of a complete system.

Nearly every kind of content can find a visual world of its own.

### 04 — About 20 seconds

But this isn’t about randomly choosing from six hundred templates.

First, it seeks to understand:

Why does this text exist?  
Who should it persuade?  
What should it explain?  
What should the audience feel?

It doesn’t search for the flashiest effect.

It searches for the clearest, most powerful expression.

### 05 — About 22 seconds

Then, language becomes rhythm.

Ideas form structures.  
Structures create movement.  
Movement becomes narrative.

An important statement can break through the frame.  
Complex data can begin to grow.  
An abstract mechanism can be taken apart, reconnected, and brought to life before your eyes.

Here, animation is no longer decoration.

Animation is the content itself.

### 06 — About 16 seconds

In the end, everything is written into one complete HTML file.

No timeline.  
No massive production pipeline.

HTML, CSS, SVG, and Canvas come together to create an animated experience that plays directly in the browser.

### 07 — About 14 seconds

Six hundred styles do not mean six hundred effects.

They mean six hundred ways of seeing the world.

Giving rhythm to data.  
Giving space to knowledge.  
Giving light and shadow to stories.  
Allowing words that could once only be read—

to finally be seen.

HTML Text to Animation.

Start with a sentence. Create a world.
