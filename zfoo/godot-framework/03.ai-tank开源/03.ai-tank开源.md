# Storyboard — AI TANK：写下第一条命令

## Materials
- `prompt/intro.md`（原稿旁白）→ 全片事实与 VO 来源：童年守鹰、坦克会自己找路/判断目标/听懂书面命令、AI TANK、手动模式（蓝主坦克 + AI 队友）、AI 模式（整队交给智能体、失败后调整再战）、35 关、倒计时内摧毁敌方基地、最多六辆作战单位、坦克与吉普组成 AI 小队、Tank Agent、两条示例命令、创建智能体、四种战斗风格（防守 / 冲锋 / 开路 / 找增益）、敌人升级（普通坦克 → 高速吉普 → 红色精英 → 双炮/四炮 Boss）、强化道具（子弹升级、攻击强化、冻结敌人、空袭支援）、对错策略、指挥官或亲自驾驶、CTA
- 项目已定视觉（`prompt/video/opening-video.md`、坦克/吉普/鹰徽/地块提示词）→ 高精度现代像素、军事科幻、正交俯视；我方蓝 / 浅绿，敌方黑坦克、白吉普、红精英与 Boss；方形鹰徽基地 + 砖墙
- 无单独参考静帧交付 → 全片 **t2v**（造型与配色对齐上述单位，不另造涂装）

## Visual Style
- Medium: High-res modern pixel art, military sci-fi top-down tank battle (not photoreal CGI, not chunky 8-bit mosaic)
- Mood: nostalgic danger → uncanny autonomy → command-and-control empowerment
- Color / light: cold gray-green battlefield; saturated blue / mint-green friendlies; black / white / red hostiles; orange-white pixel explosions; even top-down light, weak shadows
- Camera: strict orthogonal top-down (90°); smooth push/pan only; no Dutch angle, FPS, or isometric
- Motion energy: heavy tracks, fast tracer shells, crisp circular explosions; UI typing as the only “human” motion
- Aspect: 16:9

## Spine
Shot 01 stops the scroll with the childhood eagle under fire—not a feature list. Then the tank starts moving on its own, the product names itself, two play modes and the 35-stage clock land, Tank Agent turns written orders into roles, threats and pickups raise the stakes, and the close asks you to type the first command. Assumption: 16:9 store / Bilibili / YouTube intro for players who remember classic eagle-defense tank battles. Runtime ~90s.

## Assembly
01 hook → 02 twist → 03 name → 04 manual → 05 AI retry → 06 35-stage clock → 07 six-unit squad → 08 Tank Agent orders → 09 four styles → 10 bosses → 11 pickups / strategy → 12 commander or driver → 13 CTA. Total ~90s. TTS: keep the two quoted orders in Shot 08 at natural command pace (not whispered). BGM: sparse percussion under 01–02, add a pulse from 03, peak on 10, drop to almost dry for 13 so the last line is clear. Stitch on explosions / smash-cuts except 08–09 (hold the same command-panel space). No burnt-in subtitles in the video prompts; VO is separate.
